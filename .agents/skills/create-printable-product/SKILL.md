# Create a printable product

Identify who will use the product, what they will do with it, the intended paper size, and which parts the owner needs to edit. Carry forward the requested logo, theme, and product format. For a children's book, put the intended age range on the cover and use it to choose the amount of reading, detail, and pencil control each page asks for.

Use supplied references to identify visual preferences. Preserve the original logo proportions. Establish a repeatable page layout and a small palette, then vary the subjects, poses, or activities within it.

For this portfolio’s busy books, source illustrations and decorative graphics from Canva Elements. Use the supplied AJ logo as an uploaded brand asset. Search the library by subject and illustration style. Choose a consistent collection whose artwork is licensed for the intended commercial use. Build the pages in Canva with separately selectable graphics, text, and logo. Use library artwork throughout and skip custom-drawn or newly generated illustrations.

For coloring pages, test one animal in the editor first. Choose a graphic with editable fills and a distinct outline. Duplicate it, turn its interior fills white while retaining the outlines and eyes, and place the large coloring version below the small full-color guide at the top right. A matching outline and color pair from one Canva collection also works. Check that each pose and marking matches. Leave open paper where a prompt asks the child to draw, and verify any counts or color clues.

Check whether the session can select stock elements and place them in a native Canva design. If those capabilities are unavailable, prepare page copy and library search terms as needed and identify the Canva assembly still pending.

Review every page in the Canva master for individually selectable objects, then inspect the print export for clipped marks, overlapping text, logo distortion, usable coloring spaces, page count, and paper size. Keep the editable master in the owner's Canva account and export PDF Print for the finished customer download. Check the intended sale against [Canva's product-sale guidance](https://www.canva.com/help/using-canva-to-create-products-for-sale/).
